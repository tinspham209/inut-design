# Product / landing-page copy

Inputs: product name, optional primary keyword and audience; inspect actual
product data and user facts before drafting. Brand: INUT Design, Da Nang,
Vietnam. Voice: professional, modern, friendly, technically accurate Markdown;
bold important terms naturally. Emphasize substantiated value, service and CTA,
not unverifiable “best”, cheap-price or durability promises.

Use the sales-page structure when applicable (not as a compulsory blog outline):
1. Product title with keyword.
2. Concise opening value proposition.
3. Definition for new customers.
4. Design/technical preparation (cutline, resolution, file requirements when known).
5. Actual production process (do not invent lamination/finishing steps).
6. Useful comparison with related products.
7. Features and practical benefits.
8. Applications/order preparation; MOQ/time/price only when sourced.
9. Conclusion and contextual INUT CTA.

Omit unsupported sections or specifics; report missing facts outside customer
copy. The retired defaults “10 items” and “3–4 working days” are not evidence.

Product families for orientation, not an authoritative spec catalogue: Sticker
(Sheet/Die Cut/Kiss Cut/Magnet); souvenirs (acrylic keychains, pins, Acrylic
Magnet, postcard, thank/gift card, Cricket custom lighters); digital photo prints;
office (boards, envelopes, certificates, notebooks, yearbooks); marketing
(voucher, loyalty cards, flyers, catalogue, brochure, business cards); events
(poster decal, banner, standee, handheld hashtag, medals); F&B (menu, order slip,
ticket, placemat); packaging (labels, boxes, warranty seals); laptop/phone skins.
